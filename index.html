Learn AI Sports with Phoebe

Two tracks into analytics a coach will actually use

Sport got modelled before almost anything else, which means it has had longer than most fields to learn where models earn their place and where they quietly do damage. Commercial shot-quality models now train on close to a million historical shots across twenty-plus variables, and post-match expected goals predicts match outcome at around 0.656 accuracy - genuinely useful, and nowhere near the certainty the broadcast graphic implies. Meanwhile injury-risk models in the published literature range from accuracy 52% and AUC 0.52 up to AUC 0.87, are inconsistent across studies, and the reviews are blunt that these outputs are supportive information only and must never decide selection or return to play on their own. The models are weakest exactly where the stakes are highest, and that tension shapes the whole course. A director track gives sporting directors the data estate, recruitment, athlete welfare and the integrity rails. An analyst track runs one club - Rivermouth FC, three analysts, one match report a coach will read - with a live shot model where you watch the held-out number climb from 50% to 83%, and then watch one extra feature raise the headline number by eleven points while the real one falls six.
